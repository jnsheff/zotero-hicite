Citation keys Keys are pinned in each item's Extra field the first time they are needed. A changed setting applies to items that have no key yet; to re-key existing items, right-click them (or a collection) and choose "hicite: Regenerate Citation Key", "hicite: Regenerate Case Keys" or "hicite: Regenerate All Citation Keys". Export Changes apply to the next export and re-run your auto-exports. Auto-exports To add one, right-click a library or collection and choose "hicite: Auto-Export to File…", or tick "Keep updated" when exporting with the hicite format.
