Citation keys Keys are Better BibTeX's, kept in Zotero's Citation Key field: with Better BibTeX installed it makes them (from its default formula, except for legal sources, below); without it, hicite makes the same keys itself. A key never changes by itself. To re-key an item, right-click it (or a collection) and choose "hicite: Regenerate Citation Key", "hicite: Regenerate Case Keys" or "hicite: Regenerate All Citation Keys". Cases: the first party's name and the year. Statutes, bills and hearings: the Short Title, else the first word of the title, and the year. Everything else: Better BibTeX's default, author + three title words + year. Export Changes apply to the next export and re-run your auto-exports. Legal data (citation-phoenix) Auto-exports To add one, right-click a library or collection and choose "hicite: Auto-Export to File…", or tick "Keep updated" when exporting with the hicite format.
