Citation keys Keys are pinned in each item the first time they are needed (in the place chosen above; to move existing keys into Zotero's Citation Key field, right-click a collection and choose "hicite: Move Keys to Citation Key Field"). If Better BibTeX also writes that field, turn off its automatic key generation, or it can overwrite hicite's keys. A changed setting applies to items that have no key yet; to re-key existing items, right-click them (or a collection) and choose "hicite: Regenerate Citation Key", "hicite: Regenerate Case Keys" or "hicite: Regenerate All Citation Keys". Export Changes apply to the next export and re-run your auto-exports. Legal data (citation-phoenix) Auto-exports To add one, right-click a library or collection and choose "hicite: Auto-Export to File…", or tick "Keep updated" when exporting with the hicite format.
